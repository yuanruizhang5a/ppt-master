# Delegated final design decision

Stage 1 was confirmed in chat with “Confirm and go”; remaining planning was delegated. Free design is selected. No template is installed. No UI receipt is synthesized.

Three complete directions were compared within the confirmed source-order contract:

1. Source companion: 80 sequential teaching sheets, white background, navy Arial headings, teal section cues, Consolas code, amber exercise labels, source-line footer and unabridged source passages in notes. Optional image direction: flat annotated constructor illustrations resembling a teaching whiteboard; inherited palette; no images selected.
2. Lecture board: the same source sequence in approximately 90 sparse pages, dark navy background, white Arial headings and code, cyan focus marks and amber exercise cues. Optional image direction: chalk-like explanatory marks resembling a live proof lecture; inherited palette; no images selected.
3. Reading handout: the same source sequence in approximately 70 denser pages, warm white background, Cambria headings and Arial prose with Consolas code, brown section cues and black text. Optional image direction: ink-like marginal illustrations resembling an annotated printed chapter; inherited palette; no images selected.

Direction 1 best supports parallel reading of the HTML and .v files without compressing the examples too much. Its mode, visual style and optional rendering direction are project-specific customs with no preset references. Final settings: 80 slides, 4:3, text reading mode, images none, icons none, continuous execution, notes enabled, custom animations disabled, narration audio disabled, spec refinement disabled. All exercise solutions remain unfinished. Final destination: projects/course-rocq/Basics-2.pptx.
